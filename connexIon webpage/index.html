ABOUT US

Building the Leaders of Tomorrow, Today.

ConnexIon is one of the technical clubs of IIIT Sri City which revolves mostly around IoT & Embedded systems. This is an initiative taken to encourage students in IoT & other related fields.

Members of the club do take part in real time projects based on IoT and other related domains which can help them in developing new skills and gaining knowledge in a more practical way. This will also encourage peer to peer learning among the students.

OUR AIM

  • The main motto of this club is to help students gain more practical knowledge through hands-on sessions, projects, and seminars.
  • To let students work and learn about various hardware like Arduino, Raspberry Pi, NodeMCU, Sensors, Actuators, etc., and gain new skills.
  • Host guest lectures by professors from the institute or other organisations to help students understand industry-level aspects and current progress.
  • To represent the college through the club in any technical event.

Faculty Mentor

Dr. Priyanka Dwivedi

Associate Professor

Ph.D. · Indian Institute of Technology Delhi, India Associate Dean Academics (DVL) & Associate Dean Faculty (FW) priyanka.dwivedi@iiits.in

Research Works

MoS₂/Porous Silicon Nanostructures for Ethanol Sensing (2017)

Combines molybdenum disulfide and porous silicon for selective, room-temperature ethanol sensing, with wafer-scale synthesis for practical fabrication.

Key contribution: Nanomaterial-based ethanol detection for industrial and environmental monitoring.

TiO₂ Nanotube/Porous Silicon Sensor for Ethanol Detection (2017)

Studies a titanium dioxide nanotube and porous silicon sensor prototype, emphasizing sensitivity, selectivity, and scalable fabrication.

Key contribution: A sensor design exploring ethanol detection at sub-parts-per-million concentrations.

MoS₂-Based Artificial Synapse for Neuromorphic Computing (2023)

Explores an optical MoS₂ device that mimics biological synapses and brain-inspired information processing.

Key contribution: Connects semiconductor materials with artificial synapses and emerging neuromorphic computing.

Stress Classification and Vital-Sign Forecasting for IoT Health Monitoring (2022)

Uses computational analysis of health data to classify stress and forecast vital signs in IoT-based monitoring.

Key contribution: Supports connected healthcare, wearable technology, and remote health monitoring.
Research focus: Nanomaterials, semiconductor devices, chemical sensing, neuromorphic computing, and IoT healthcare—connecting electronic materials and device development with practical sensing, computing, and health applications.

A place to explore, build, and belong.

ConnexIon is more than a club — it’s a place where curiosity turns into hands-on experience, ideas become real projects, and learning happens together. Whether you’re just starting out or already passionate about IoT, electronics, and embedded systems, there’s room for you to experiment, collaborate, and grow.

Step beyond the classroom, meet people who share your curiosity, take on exciting challenges, and discover what you’re capable of creating. You don’t need to know everything before you begin — just bring your enthusiasm and willingness to learn.

ALUMNI SPEAKS

Insights from those who came before

Kannan M

Associate Engineer, Caterpillar

Batch of ’25

Connexion will always be special to me, it’s the place where I first learned leadership, management, and administration, and it’s also where I found all my closest friends. What started as a fun space to share ideas soon turned into a journey of growth, filled with late-night planning, endless discussions, and little wins that we celebrated together. Everything I learned here has played a huge role in shaping who I am today and has brought me to where I stand now.

Adi Narayana

Assistant Engineer, Planys Technologies

Batch of ’25

Being part of Connexion during my academics gave me the chance to explore, experiment, organize, and communicate better, not just on the technical side, but also in understanding people, sharing ideas, and working in a team. Connexion is that place where you can start building from the very beginning. It’s not just about projects or events, it’s a gateway to explore new areas, opportunities, and networks that can open many routes for your future. Be enthusiastic, make the most of it, and I hope you’ll have a wonderful time being part of it.

Sreyas Nilagiri

Pursuing Masters, Technical University of Munich

Batch of ’24

When I joined Connexion, we were just 8 members organizing hackathons. With support from professors, we connected with industries, gained funding, and hands-on experience. That journey helped me get into one of the best universities for electronics—and if I can do it, anyone can. My advice: don’t wait for things to fall into place. Create your own path, experiment, and don’t be afraid to try. I hope the club helps you grow and achieve your goals as it did for me.

Sharan Karthick

Former Club Lead, ConnexIon

Batch of ’26

My journey with ConnexIon has been one of the most memorable parts of my life at IIITS. What started as an opportunity to explore IoT, circuits, and embedded systems turned into something much bigger (and, at times, a fair bit more chaotic than expected!). From working on projects and organising events, from TechTales to HawkinsTrail, to eventually leading the club, I've learned a lot beyond just the technical side of things. Be it figuring out why something refuses to work five minutes before a demo, or somehow turning a random idea into an actual event, there's never really been a dull moment! ConnexIon taught me how to work with people, take responsibility, deal with the occasional chaos, and turn ideas into something real. More than anything, it gave me a space to experiment, make mistakes, learn, and grow alongside some absolutely brilliant people. Looking back, I'm genuinely glad I got to be part of this journey. And I hope ConnexIon continues to inspire curious minds to build, explore, break things (hopefully not too expensive to fix!), and push beyond what they learn in the classroom. After all, that's where the real fun begins. Cheers!
